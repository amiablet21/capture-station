What it is

  • The Stock sheet, nothing added. The same rows the desktop Stock page shows (the live Linnworks inventory at Digital World Shop), the same columns in the same order, the same sort (In stock, highest first; tap a header to sort by another column), the same colours: red gutter when a SKU is out, red Available when it is below the minimum.
  • Images are the item's Linnworks picture, loaded straight from the Linnworks CDN like the owner's phone page does today.
  • Search covers SKU, title and barcode, as on the desktop.
  • Read-only. No inline editing of counts, min or cost on his phone; those stay on the desktop. Cost is not shown.
  • No sales dollars anywhere.

Anywhere he goes

Same as your phone today: Tailscale on his phone and an invite to the tailnet (or a share of just the Capture Station PC), his own QR from the desktop, "Add to Home Screen". The Capture Station PC has to be on with the app running, because the page is served by the app and only the tailnet can reach it. His token is separate from yours, so it can be revoked on its own, and it only opens this page, never your Overview.

Desktop side

  • One new page (supply.html) on the same :8484 server behind a second token, reading the existing /stock payload the owner's phone already uses (the 60-second-cached inventory projection), so there is no new Linnworks traffic.
  • Settings › Pages on this station gains "Supply phone" with its QR.
