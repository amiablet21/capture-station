What it is

  • One page, one list. The Running-low computation run over every SKU, not only the ones inside the lead: dial = days on hand ÷ (lead + cover), sold over 30 days, on hand = shelf + WFS, Order = pace × (lead + cover) − on hand rounded up to 5, Faster = last 14 days vs the 16 before. Green rows say Fine. No sales in 30 days shows a dash.
  • Chips: All, Running low (inside the lead), Soon (inside lead + cover). Soonest-out first inside each.
  • Ignore is the Overview's 14-day ignore, shared with the desktop, so you both see the same list. Order is a number, not a button: it tells him how many.
  • Tap a row to open it: shelf / WFS / in orders / cost, then each channel SKU with the channel's listed price, listed qty and WFS flag, from the channel-mapping rows the desktop already caches. "Not listed" where there is no mapping. Cost shows only if the station has the Cost tick.
  • No sales dollars anywhere. Units, prices and costs only.

Anywhere he goes

Same as your phone today: Tailscale on his phone and an invite to the tailnet (or a share of just the Capture Station PC), his own QR from the desktop, "Add to Home Screen". The Capture Station PC has to be on with the app running, because the page is served by the app and only the tailnet can reach it. His token is separate from yours, so it can be revoked on its own, and it only opens this page, never your Overview.

Desktop side

  • One new page (supply.html) on the same :8484 server behind a second token, one endpoint (/supply: the Running-low pass over all SKUs plus each item's channel rows, cached like the Overview), and the existing ignore route.
  • Settings › Pages on this station gains "Supply phone" with its QR.
