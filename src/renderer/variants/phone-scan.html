Supply phone — every product as a Running-low row, plus a listing check

A phone page for your supply manager, served by Capture Station the same way your own phone page is, but with its own token and no sales dollars. Products is the Running-low card from the Overview, extended to every SKU: the same dial (days on hand out of the 7-day lead + 21 cover days), sold / on hand, Faster, and Order n computed the same way. Search takes a link shared from the Walmart (or eBay / Temu) app and says whether we sell that listing, opening the item card: stock, cost, and every channel SKU with its listed price and qty. Tapping Order anywhere adds the line to To order.

What he sees, and where it comes from

  • Products is the Running-low computation run over every SKU instead of only the ones inside the lead time: dial = days on hand ÷ (lead + cover), sold over 30 days, on hand = shelf + WFS, Order = pace × (lead + cover) − on hand rounded up to 5, Faster = last 14 days vs the 16 before. Green rows say Fine instead of an Order button. A SKU with no sales in 30 days shows a dash.
  • Sorting and chips. Soonest-out first. Three chips only: All, Running low (inside the lead), Soon (inside lead + cover). Search covers SKU and title.
  • Search pulls the item id out of a pasted link (walmart.com/ip/<id>, ebay.com/itm/<id>, Temu goods_id) or takes a bare id, and matches it against the channel-mapping rows the app already caches: the marketplace reference id Linnworks keeps per listing, the same id the desktop uses to open the product page when you click a channel SKU. No Linnworks call per check, so it answers instantly. Three answers: selling it (our SKU, price, stock, days, order), ours but not linked to an inventory item, not ours.
  • Item card = the dial row again, the warehouse numbers (on hand, shelf, WFS, in orders), cost and retail from Linnworks, condition siblings (-OPENBOX / -USED / -SCRAP, or the New parent), and the listings: channel SKU, the channel's listed price, listed qty, WFS flag, eBay item number. "Not listed" when there is no mapping.
  • No sales dollars anywhere. He gets units, prices and costs, never revenue. If cost should also be hidden from him, it is one tick on the desktop (the same Cost tick the Stock page uses).
  • Ignore is the Overview's 14-day ignore, shared with the desktop so you both see the same list.
  • To order: the Order taps collect into a list with +/− and the last cost. Send message builds a plain text order (SKU × qty per line, units total) and opens the phone's share sheet, so it goes out by Messages, WhatsApp or email; a tick adds each line's stock on hand to the text. Lines ride the shared folder so the desktop can show them too.

One tap from the Walmart app

Copy the link, open the page, paste: three taps. Or one: on iPhone a two-step Siri Shortcut sits in the share sheet of the Walmart app ("Search in Capture Station"), takes the shared link and opens the phone page with the answer already showing. On Android the page itself can be a share target. Both are a few minutes to set up on his phone.

Anywhere he goes

Same as your phone today: Tailscale on his phone and an invite to the tailnet (or a share of just the Capture Station PC), his own QR from the desktop, "Add to Home Screen". The Capture Station PC has to be on with the app running, because the page is served by the app and only the tailnet can reach it. His token is separate from yours, so it can be revoked on its own, and it only opens this page, never your Overview.

Desktop side

  • A second phone page (supply.html) on the same :8484 server, behind a second token, with three endpoints: /supply/products (the Running-low pass over all SKUs, cached like the Overview), /supply/search?u=… (the pasted link or id, matched against the channel-mapping and inventory caches the desktop already holds), and /supply/order for the To-order lines.
  • Settings › Pages on this station gains "Supply phone" with its QR, and a "show cost on the supply phone" tick.
