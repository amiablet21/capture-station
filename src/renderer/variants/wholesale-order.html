Same bones as WFS Shipments: green-headed sheet, note under it, live total in the footer, past records in white cards on the gray column. Unit $ and the whole shipping row can stay blank; a blank price shows as “no price” on the card, never as $0. The “634 avail” hint sits where the line total will go and turns amber if the qty typed exceeds it.
