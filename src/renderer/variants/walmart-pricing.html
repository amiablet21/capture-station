1 · Walmart → Pricing: every listing at a glance

A third tab beside List and Uploads. One row per Walmart listing: your price, whether you hold the Buy Box (and the winning price when you don't), Walmart's suggested price, the repricer strategy with its min / max, and incentive programs. The tiles and chips filter to what needs a look. Buy Box data is fetched in the background (Walmart allows 2 calls a minute) and shows its age. Edits don't go out one by one; they collect in the bar at the bottom.
