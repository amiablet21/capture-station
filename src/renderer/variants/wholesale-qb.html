Wholesale — laid out like a QuickBooks invoice

The form you already know from QuickBooks Online: customer and email across the top, balance due top right, billing address and dates, the product/service line grid, message box on the left and totals on the right, dark action bar along the bottom. Two things ride inside it that QuickBooks doesn't have: an In stock column on every line, and a stock note under the totals saying what Save takes off the warehouse.

Product/Service is the Linnworks SKU (suggestions come from live inventory with stock and the price this customer last paid); Description autofills from the item title and stays editable. Rate is optional: leave it blank and the amount shows 0.00, the same way QuickBooks does. "Scan barcode" beside Add lines puts a scanner box on the next empty line. Terms and due date are just stored on the invoice; nothing in the app chases payment. "Amount received" lets you mark a paid-on-pickup order so Balance due reads 0.00.
