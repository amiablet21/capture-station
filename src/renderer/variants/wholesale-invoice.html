Wholesale — an invoice you fill in, not a shipment log

The dialog is a workspace with two tabs: the order you're writing, and every order written before. The order itself is laid out like the paper invoice the customer gets: bill-to block, invoice number, ruled item lines, totals at the bottom right. What leaves the shelf is spelled out beside the totals before you save.

The item picker remembers the last price this customer paid per SKU (“last @118.00”) and pre-fills it — you overtype or leave it blank. Invoice #, prices, shipping and memo are all optional; only the items and their qty matter to stock. The dashed underline on the invoice number means click-to-type; it auto-suggests the next number after the last one saved.
