How it flows
0Linnworks carries no GTINs — find from eBay lifts the UPC off your live listing's item specifics and saves it into the Linnworks barcode for good (also feeds the Temu export). No UPC found = the search falls back to the product name.
1Tick the SKUs (up to 50) — New and Open Box both — and press Search on Walmart: the UPC list is copied and the pane opens Seller Central's item search, ready to paste.
2Walmart shows the matching catalog items. Press Add offer on each — the cheat sheet below has the SKU, price, and qty to fill in.
3Because the offer SKU is your Linnworks SKU, Linnworks links the new listing on its next channel scan — no mapping needed.
