Wholesale — stock first, money optional

Same dialog family as Stock history: title with a mono reference, a gray subline, pill filters, the stat strip, and pill-tagged rows. The order is just “these units left the shelf for this customer.” Price and shipping live in a collapsed section at the bottom for the times you want them on record, and nothing about them is ever required.

The customer pill is the only thing you pick besides the SKUs, and even that can stay on “Customer…” for a walk-in. Invoice, date and note are pills you click to type into. Each line is a Stock history row turned around: the red pill is the amount leaving, the stepper changes it, the text shows the shelf count before and after.
