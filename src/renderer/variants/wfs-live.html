Where each number comes from. Sellable, held, on the way and checking-in come from Walmart's WFS inventory and inbound-shipment APIs, refreshed when the page opens and every 15 minutes. 30-day WFS sales come from the orders the Overview already reads (sales shipped from the WFS location). "Send" = enough for the cover target, minus what's at WFS and on the way, capped at what's in your warehouse, rounded to 5.
