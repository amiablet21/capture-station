2 · Find it on Walmart by name, then set condition, price and stock

One SKU at a time (left). The app searches Walmart's catalog for the product name and lists Walmart's best picks, each with a View on Walmart ↗ link. The pick whose model, storage and colour agree with your SKU is pre-selected and labelled; the chips show what matches and what doesn't. Confirm the right one, choose the condition, price and starting stock, then Pick & next. The search box is editable if the first try misses.

4 · Sent: results come back per SKU

All offers go to Walmart in one upload. Walmart takes anywhere from a few minutes to a few hours, so the dialog can be closed. The app keeps checking and shows a toast when the results are in. Problems are shown in plain words with a fix-and-resend for that row only.
