The DropShip view — a chip beside WFS on the Stock page
Click the DropShip chip and the sheet becomes the program: which SKUs dropship, their pad, and — the point of the whole view — how many units you've been renting from the supplier lately. Rows heat up as the numbers say "stop dropshipping, buy it."

How it reads: Per day = units/day over the trailing 30 days, arrow = last week vs the month (▲ speeding up, → steady, ▼ slowing down); hover for the raw Today/7/30/90 counts. Past month = units dropshipped in the last 30 days. Action does the math for you: Buy ≈ velocity × (cover + lead time) − on hand and Min = velocity × lead time × 1.5, with lead time and cover days as two small settings (defaults 7 and 21). The Pad is the one control: the number of units the app keeps at DropShip (click to edit). Set it to 0 when no supplier can source it — the listing goes dark instead of overselling. Removing a SKU from the program entirely is a right-click on the row.
Enrolling a SKU — from the normal stock view
Any SKU's row in the regular Stock list gets a small "+ DropShip" affordance (row hover). One click enrolls it with a default pad of 10 — no separate screen.
