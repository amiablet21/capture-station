Stock history — redesign

Same data the dialog has today (the logged changes plus 30 days of Linnworks sales), laid out so you can read it faster. What changes from the current one:
  1. A signed quantity column. Today every row says "1 unit" and you work out the direction from the pill colour. Here it's −1 or +1, lined up so you can scan it.
  2. Grouped by day. The date shows once in a sticky header with that day's in/out total, not on every row.
  3. Filter chips with counts (Sold 63 · Returned 2 · Edited 1) replace the "All actions" dropdown, so you see the mix before you click.
  4. Better tiles. "Last touched" moves into the subtitle. Its tile becomes Sold · 30 days with a daily sparkline, and Out · 7 days shows in and out together.
  5. Lighter labels. A coloured dot and a normal-case word instead of a big all-caps pill. The colour still carries the meaning, with less shouting.
