Returns sheet v2 — the flow, then two looks

Top: how a return moves through the entry row. Below: two polish directions for the same sheet — V1 keeps the RETURNS-2 green band but calms it and tidies the columns; V2 trades the band for the app's own quiet header so Returns matches Stock/History.

The receiving flow

1Type the PO#

First cell of the green row. Enter or clicking away starts the lookup.

2Linnworks fills the row

Customer, Tracking #, SKU, Price — and Dispute $ mirrors the price. Ordered lines show as chips; click the one that came back.

No order found → hint says "enter by hand", row stays typeable.

3Pick the condition

New / Open box / Used / Scrap. The mapping engine auto-matches the landing SKU: → OPEN-BOX-TABA9+64GB

No listing yet → pick an existing one, or Create opens the New SKU sheet prefilled; creating it maps + routes this return.

4Adjust anything

Units, Price, Dispute $ (clear it when there's no dispute), initials, notes — case: 12345 in Notes opens a dispute card.

5Receive

Enter from any later cell, or the ✓ button. Stock +qty lands on the condition SKU in Linnworks, the row drops into the log, and a multi-item PO stays loaded on its next line.

V1 — polished RETURNS-2 recommended

Keeps your sheet's identity. Changes: the two big cards collapse into chips on the bar (click to expand) so the sheet starts at the top; softer green with zebra rows; condition as a pastel pill with the landing SKU underneath; money right-aligned, settlement bold only when it exists; a one-line hint strip under the entry row replaces the tall in-cell stacking.
