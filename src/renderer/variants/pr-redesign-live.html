If I designed the pricing sheet from scratch

Three real concepts, not column shuffles. The common moves: one price per cell (listing SKUs get out of the way until you hover or expand), Walmart as the anchor with each other channel showing its % above/below it, and sold-through as a glanceable heat dot instead of ×0 noise on every line. Everything here is clickable where it matters.
1 · Terminal

Built for scanning 150 products fast. One line per product, one price per channel, green/red delta vs Walmart. The "+1" pill expands extra listings on that channel; gaps whisper until you hover. Filter chips answer the real questions: what's mismatched, what's unlisted, what's actually selling.

3 · Clean sheet

The spreadsheet, decluttered. One price per channel column; a heat dot carries sold-through (grey none · amber some · red hot); a small ² marks a second listing (hover the price to see the listing SKUs). Click a price and it becomes a proper cell editor with steppers — shown live on X230's eBay price. A footer keeps the totals in view.
