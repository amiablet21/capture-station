Entry row decluttered — one row, one strip

The entry row stays ONE line tall, always: inputs only. Everything contextual — the ordered lines, where stock lands, the missing-listing fix — moves onto a single strip right under it, which only appears once a PO matches. The dropdown slims to dots + names; the strip already says where stock lands.
