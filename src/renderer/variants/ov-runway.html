Tick = the 7-day lead · solid = days on hand · hatched = what the order adds
Every SKU gets a runway: the solid part is how many days are covered right now (red under 5, amber under 10), the hatched green part is what the Send / Order adds, out to the target (30 days at WFS; the 7-day lead plus 14 days of cover for Running low, with a tick at the lead - when the solid part stops short of the tick, it runs out before the order lands). A short hatch means there isn't enough stock to fill it (X230-256GB-GRAY: only 3 on the shelf). The quantity rides inside the key. Rows are hairline-separated, so the column reads as one list.
