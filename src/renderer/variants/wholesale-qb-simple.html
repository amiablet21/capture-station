Wholesale — the invoice, in the app's own styling

The QuickBooks invoice layout (customer and number up top, lines, note left, totals right) drawn with the app's own pieces: its dialog, its buttons, its inputs, its table headers, its green. No terms, due date, email, discount, amount received, attachments or recurring. This is inventory management first: price and shipping are optional extras for your own records.

The barcode icon on each line opens the serial popup below; it turns green with a count once serials are logged, amber while fewer than the qty. Only SKU and Qty are required. Rate, shipping and note are optional; a line with no rate simply doesn't count toward the total, and Save never asks for them. The customer's saved details sit in one gray line under the box with an “edit” link.

One scan box, newest on top. Each serial is written the moment Enter lands, so closing the popup or the app loses nothing. A repeat scan is flagged and not added. Logging more serials than the line's qty is allowed but the count badge turns amber. Export CSV writes invoice, customer, SKU, serial and time, one row per serial; the invoice's Print includes the serials under each line.
