Wholesale — the invoice, in the app's own styling

The QuickBooks invoice layout (customer and number up top, lines, note left, totals right) drawn with the app's own pieces: its dialog, its buttons, its inputs, its table headers, its green. No terms, due date, email, discount, amount received, attachments or recurring. This is inventory management first: price and shipping are optional extras for your own records.
