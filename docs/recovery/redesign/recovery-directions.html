Three ways to make Recovery readable

Each direction is drawn with your Nov 1 – Nov 10 WFS report from the screenshot. Dollar amounts and SKU names on refunds are examples. Tabs and rows in the frames are clickable.

What makes the page hard to read today

  • Tabs inside tabsRecovery › WFS Inventory › Not received is three levels deep. The 41 shipments that need you are hidden behind two clicks.
  • Import is a screen, not an actionYou visit a whole tab to drop a file, then go to another tab to see what it changed.
  • Eleven number columns, mostly dashesLost, Found, Removed, Returned, Moved are empty on almost every row but take half the width.
  • The answer isn't on screenYou want to know “did Walmart lose anything?”. The table makes you add 75 + 48 − 25 in your head to check the 98.
  • Signs flipSold shows as −25 in Walmart's file but Lost/Removed are positive. Every out-flow should read the same way.
  • Shipped vs received is a faint tintTab A9+ shows 48 received with nothing logged as shipped in those days. Today that is a small amber dot in the legend.

My recommendation: A + B

Use A's structure for the whole page (To do · Refunds · WFS stock · Profit, one Import button) and B for the WFS stock tab. It fixes the two biggest problems, depth and arithmetic, and the To do list gives the counts on the tabs a single meaning.

  1. Build the new tab bar and a single Import that reads both file types.
  2. Build the To do home from data the app already has (refund windows, shipment ages, shipped vs received).
  3. Rebuild WFS stock as B. Keep a “Show every column” switch so nothing Walmart sends is lost.
  4. Refunds and Profit keep their current tables for now, then get the same pass.
